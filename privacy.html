Privacy Policy

Jhin Uploader · last updated 20 November 2026

What this app is

Jhin Uploader is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to, and read information about, the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Jhin Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using Jhin Uploader you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it uses

  • The Google sign-in permission (an OAuth token) the owner grants, so it can act on the owner's channels.
  • Information about the owner's own channels and videos, such as titles, upload status and statistics.
  • Public video information (search results, playlists, video titles and view counts) that the owner looks up for their own research. It is shown only to the owner and is not published or shared.

Where the data goes

Everything stays on the owner's computer. The app sends data only to YouTube, to carry out uploads and reads the owner asked for. It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone else, and it does not use Google user data for advertising.

Jhin Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and tracking

Jhin Uploader has no website accounts and places no cookies or trackers on anyone's device. It stores nothing on the devices of anyone other than its owner.

Keeping and deleting data

The sign-in permission is kept, encrypted, on the owner's computer until it is disconnected. Video and channel information read from YouTube is about the owner's own channels and is used only for the owner's own uploads and reports. Access can be removed at any time through the Google security settings page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Removing it stops all access immediately.
