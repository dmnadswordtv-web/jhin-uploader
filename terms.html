Who can use it

Jhin Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, for the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.

YouTube

Jhin Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data follows the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

It uploads videos the owner made, sets their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reads back their status and statistics. It does not like, comment, subscribe or otherwise act on anyone else's content, and it does not download or copy other people's videos.
